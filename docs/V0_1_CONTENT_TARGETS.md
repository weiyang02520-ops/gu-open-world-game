# V0.1 Content Targets

> Purpose: give Sol enough concrete content to build a real vertical slice instead of only frameworks.
> All names below are placeholders unless source-backed; label GAME_EXTENSION.

## 1. Local region shape

Create one compact Southern Border-inspired local region with:

- 1 main settlement / governing power center;
- 2–4 small settlements or camps;
- 1 merchant hub;
- 1 independent Gu Master gathering area;
- 1 forest resource zone;
- 1 beast valley;
- 1 river/wetland zone;
- 1 abandoned mine/cave system;
- 1 dangerous route segment;
- 1 safer trade route;
- 1 hidden inheritance/study location;
- 15–30 additional sublocations/resource points.

Use hierarchical location IDs and deterministic travel times.

## 2. Faction archetypes

At least 3 active groups:

### Local Authority / Clan
Needs:
- security;
- stability;
- prestige;
- resource control.

Offices:
- leader;
- elder/council;
- security officer;
- steward.

Blocs:
- security hardliners;
- pragmatic stabilizers.

### Merchant Association
Needs:
- revenue;
- route safety;
- supply;
- reliability.

Offices:
- chief steward;
- quartermaster;
- route coordinator.

Blocs:
- conservative traders;
- expansion/profit faction.

### Independent Gu Master / Hunter Association
Needs:
- mutual protection;
- information;
- hunting/resources;
- recruitment.

Looser governance.

Optional fourth/fifth faction:
- healer/herbalist group;
- village militia;
- underground criminal network.

Do not use canon names unless provenance is established.

## 3. Important NPC archetypes

Target 15–30 important actors across V0.1.

Minimum variety:

- gatherer;
- merchant;
- quartermaster;
- security officer;
- faction leader;
- conservative elder;
- ambitious officer;
- investigator;
- healer;
- hunter;
- hostile opportunist;
- information broker;
- caravan leader;
- independent cultivator;
- potential ally;
- potential rival.

Give each:
- stable ID;
- faction/member status;
- cultivation;
- owned Gu subset;
- economic role;
- needs/goals;
- relationships;
- risk tolerance;
- political interests if relevant.

Avoid cloning stats with different names.

## 4. Gu content

Target 30–50 definitions.

Organize into functional groups:

- attack;
- movement;
- perception;
- defense;
- healing;
- concealment;
- tracking;
- resource gathering;
- cultivation support;
- utility.

At least 20 Gu should have feeding requirements that create distinct resource demand.

Exact canon Gu only when source-backed.
Otherwise use GAME_EXTENSION placeholders.

## 5. Resource families

### Gu food
10–15 resource types.

### Refinement
20–30 materials.

### Common ecology
10–20 plant/beast/mineral resources.

### Strategic
3–5 rare resources important to factions/cultivation.

## 6. Markets

3–4 distinct market/supply hubs:

- main settlement market;
- merchant warehouse/exchange;
- outer gathering camp;
- optional independent/black-market-like supply point if scope remains clean.

Each should have different:
- initial stock;
- demand;
- faction control;
- taxes/discounts;
- route exposure.

## 7. Routes

5–8 route segments.

Include:
- safe short route;
- longer cheap route;
- risky shortcut;
- resource route;
- caravan corridor.

Route risk changes from world events.

## 8. Cases / political content

At least:

- violence accusation case;
- theft/economic dispute case;
- optional false identity/rumor case.

Each should be able to:
- open from incomplete info;
- progress;
- become disputed;
- close or remain unresolved.

## 9. Missions

At least 5 mission templates total, with 2+ exercised heavily:

- gather/supply;
- investigate;
- deliver message;
- watch/contact target;
- guard/escort;
- recruit/contact.

Mission completion must use ordinary world state.

## 10. Cultivation content

V0.1 active:

- Rank1 Initial/Middle/Upper/Peak;
- one or more cultivation methods;
- safe/forced breakthrough;
- one study/inheritance source;
- 1–3 active paths for attainment proof.

## 11. Killer-move content

At least:

- one player-discoverable multi-Gu move;
- one NPC-known move if useful;
- one unstable candidate;
- one illegal combination test.

## 12. Day-10 state summaries

Final summary should include:

- player cultivation;
- essence;
- Gu conditions;
- killer move;
- inventory/wealth;
- relationships;
- reputations;
- standings;
- open/closed cases;
- faction memberships;
- market stock/prices;
- resource node health;
- caravan/shipment outcomes;
- major rumors/beliefs;
- important NPC status/goals;
- major event-log causal chain.

## 13. Content quality rule

Prefer 20 meaningful locations over 100 empty names.
Prefer 15 distinct NPCs over 50 stat clones.
Prefer 30 Gu with real feeding/effect roles over 100 inert definitions.

V0.1 content exists to prove interacting simulation, not database size.
